# C3.3 — the notice: a plan, proposed and not agreed

**Status:** drafted 2026-09-29 (fifty-third session) for the maintainer. **Nothing
here is built.** Answer the four questions at the end, then build.

C3's exit: *a reader returning after a new sequence sees what it changed for their
goals and plan, without asking.* C3.1 saved the plan and C3.2 serves the report
(`GET /api/me/profiles/{p}/since`, ADR 0037). C3.3 is the half a reader sees.

## What the reader sees

### 1. Home, on the profile's row

One line under the profile's name, and only when a sequence has come out since
their last plan:

> **New since your plan:** Anchored in Faith (Global 4.8.0) · 3 changes touch it · **See what changed →**

The link goes to the plan screen. When the patch touches nothing in their plan it
says so rather than staying silent, because silence looks the same as "up to date":

> **New since your plan:** Anchored in Faith (Global 4.8.0) · nothing in it touches your plan

There is no line when the plan is up to date or has never been run. The line
loads after the row does, so Home never waits on it.

### 2. The plan screen, above the saved plan

A panel above "Worked out on … against …", shown only when there is something
new:

```
Since this plan: sequence 14 → 18

Your plan    Same cost: 1,230 Serum over 28 days
             (or: 1,230 → 1,100 Serum · 28 → 24 days)

What changed for you
  Lucia: Inverse Crown · Abyssal Lament      skill costs re-read, 14 steps
  Lucia: Inverse Crown · Dusklit Severance   skill costs re-read, 14 steps
  Ashfall Approach                           Rough Ore drop 1.4 → 1.6
  ▸ every line (32)

[ Re-plan on sequence 18 ]
```

- **Grouped, not listed.** On real data, 28 of 32 lines were one reading of
  one skill curve. Upgrade changes are grouped by entity and track and said in
  one line. Every other change (a stage, a shop row, an item, the day
  boundary) gets its own line: name, what moved, before → after. The raw lines
  sit behind a toggle, like the plan's own "how it was worked out".
- **The cost line is two fresh solves, not the saved plan** (ADR 0037). It is
  labelled "your plan" because both sides use today's inventory. The saved
  plan below it still shows what the reader was shown at the time.
- **A refused side says why**, in the solver's words: "On sequence 18 your
  goals can't be planned: …". A patch that removes the only source of
  something is the case this report most needs to tell.
- **Re-plan** runs the form as it stands, the same button the form already
  has. The new plan is saved, `/since` comes back up to date, and the panel and
  the Home line go away. **Nothing else dismisses them.**

## What gets built

| Piece | Where | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `entity`, `track`/`toState` on an upgrade change | `ChangeForYouView`, `SinceController` | Grouping needs to know whose track a step is on. The server has the definition and the page does not, at the latest sequence, without a fetch per entity. Null for anything that is not an upgrade. **A new optional field**, so the page and the API may deploy apart |
| `getSince`, `Since` wire type | `frontend/src/api/client.ts` | New fields optional, as every wire type is |
| `groupChanges(changes)` | a pure function beside `roster/tracks.ts` | Names a track the way the goal and roster screens already do. Unit-tested on the 14 → 18 shape |
| `SinceNotice` | `routes/PlanView.tsx` | The panel; invalidated with `savedPlan` on every plan run |
| The row line | `routes/Home.tsx`, `ProfileRow` | One `/since` query per profile, not blocking the row |

**Tests.** `groupChanges` on its own. A Home test with the line shown, with the
"nothing touches it" wording, and absent. A plan screen test showing the panel,
a refused side, and the panel gone after a re-plan. A backend test for the new
fields.

**Driven, as every screen is (C2's rule).** Use the local database and
`rehearsal-s6`, with a plan pinned by `POST /plan?version=14`: it gives the real
14 → 18 report, 32 changes. Drive at 375 px and 1280 px. Then re-plan on 18 so
the rehearsal account is left as it was.

**Exit met when:** a reader with a saved plan on an older sequence opens Home,
sees the line without asking, follows it, and reads what changed, all on
production. The maintainer's screen is the proof, as it was for C1.

## Four questions for the maintainer

1. **Does Home show the count, or only that a sequence is out?** The count needs
   `/since`. That is two solves (up to ~4 s, cached after the first) per
   out-of-date profile on the first Home visit after a patch. "A new sequence
   is out" alone needs no solve. **Recommended: the count, loaded without
   blocking.** The count is what makes the line worth reading, and it costs
   solves once per patch.
2. **May the reader dismiss the notice without re-planning?** **Recommended:
   no.** A re-plan is one click, and a dismissed notice leaves a stale plan on
   screen that looks current.
3. **Is "nothing in it touches your plan" worth a line?** **Recommended: yes**,
   on Home and as a one-line panel. Otherwise a reader cannot tell "the patch
   missed you" from "we did not check".
4. **Does the saved plan below the panel stay as it is?** **Recommended: yes**.
   It is what they were shown, labelled with its sequence. The panel says what
   moved, and re-planning replaces it.
