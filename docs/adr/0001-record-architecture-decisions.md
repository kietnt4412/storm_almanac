# 1. Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-02

## Context

Two tracks, thirteen phases, and roughly eight months at a realistic pace. By
phase 7 the reasoning behind a phase-1 decision will be gone, and the temptation
under deadline will be to relitigate rather than reread.

## Decision

Keep an ADR per decision that constrains later work. Every record ends with a
**reversal trigger**: the observation that would make this decision wrong.

## Consequences

Writing "I would reverse this if X" forces the alternative to be understood
rather than dismissed, and it makes the "what I would do differently" section at
the end of the project a compilation rather than an act of memory.

## Reversal trigger

None. If this folder stops being written to, the project has stopped being
designed.
